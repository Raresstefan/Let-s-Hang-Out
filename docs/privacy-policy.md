# Privacy Policy for Hang Out

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Hang Out" mobile and web application ("Hang Out", "the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. By creating an account and using Hang Out, you agree to the practices described here.

> **Contact:** For any privacy question or request, email **letshangout45@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Who we are

Hang Out is a social application that helps people organize and join in-person social activities. The app is operated by an individual developer ("we"). We are the data controller for the information described below.

## 2. What data we collect

We collect only the data needed to run the service:

**Account & identity (via email/password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In):**
- Your name (with Apple, only if you choose to share it)
- Your email address (with Apple, this may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email")
- Your Google profile photo (Google sign-in only)
- A unique account identifier
- If you use email/password, Firebase Authentication processes your password and
  sends verification and password-reset emails.

**Profile information you provide:**
- Display name, bio and interests
- A profile photo, if you upload one from your device (it replaces your Google photo, if any)
- The date and version of the Terms of Service you accepted

**Content you create:**
- Activities you create (title, description, place, time, category). Older versions also allowed activity cover images; previously uploaded covers may still be retained.
- Activities and circles (groups) you join, like, or leave
- Friend relationships and friend/circle requests
- Group chat messages you send
- Ratings you give to activities you attended

**Safety data:**
- Reports you submit (who or what you reported, the reason, and any details you add)
- Users you block

**Usage and device data (Firebase Analytics):**
- Which screens you open and which buttons or actions you use in the app
- Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language) and an app-instance identifier
- Approximate location (country/region/city) that Google derives from your IP address. We do **not** request or collect precise (GPS) location.

Photos are collected **only** when you choose a picture to upload; the app does not otherwise access your camera or photo library. We do **not** access your contacts. We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, we do **not** show ads, and we do **not** sell your data.

## 3. How we use your data

We use your data solely to:
- Authenticate you and maintain your account
- Show you activities and let you create, join, and rate them
- Enable friends, circles, and group chat
- Display your profile (name, photo, bio, interests) to other users
- Keep the community safe: filter offensive language, review reports, enforce blocks, and remove abusive content and users
- Understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve it (analytics)

## 4. Legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data:
- to **perform the contract** with you (providing the app and its features);
- on our **legitimate interests** in keeping users safe (reports, blocks, moderation) and in understanding and improving the app (analytics);
- to **comply with legal obligations** where applicable.

## 5. Third-party services (sub-processors)

Hang Out is built on Google Firebase. Your data is stored and processed by:

- **Google Firebase Authentication**, **Google Sign-In** and **Sign in with Apple** (Apple Inc.): sign-in. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **Google Cloud Firestore**: your profile (including an uploaded profile photo), activities, ratings, friends, circles, chat messages, reports, blocks, and terms acceptance.
- **Google Cloud Storage for Firebase**: activity cover images uploaded using an older version, if retained. The current version no longer uploads activity covers.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase**: usage and device data described in section 2.

Google processes this data on our behalf as a sub-processor and may store it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

The SDKs integrated in the app that handle personal data are: `firebase_core`, `firebase_auth`, `google_sign_in`, `cloud_firestore`, and `firebase_analytics`.

## 6. Data sharing & visibility

- Your **profile** (name, photo, bio, interests) is visible to other signed-in users.
- **Activities** you create, including any previously uploaded covers that remain available, are visible to other users. Members of an activity can see who else is going.
- **Chat messages** are visible to the members of that group chat.
- **Reports** are visible only to us. The person you report is not told who reported them.
- **Blocks** are not announced to the blocked user.
- Activity hosts can remove members from their activities.
- We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes, and we do not sell it. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

## 7. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete it as described in section 8. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for a limited period set in our Analytics settings and is then deleted automatically. Reports, and the content they refer to, may be kept for as long as needed to handle them and to protect users' safety, and no longer than required for that purpose.

## 8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app: **Profile → Delete account**. Email/password accounts must verify their email before entering the app and confirm their password before deletion. This deletes your profile (name, photo, bio, interests, terms acceptance), removes your friend links, removes you from activities and circles you joined (including your likes and ratings), and deletes your sign-in record. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Hang Out's Sign in with Apple authorization, so the app no longer appears under your Apple ID's "Sign in with Apple" settings.

What stays:
- Activities and circles you created, and messages you sent in group chats, stay visible to the other members so their plans and conversations are not broken. They are no longer linked to a profile.
- Reports you submitted or that concern you, and your block list, may be kept for safety reasons as described in section 7.

If you can't access the app, see https://raresstefan.github.io/Let-s-Hang-Out/delete-account or email **letshangout45@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my account" from the address you signed in with. Requests are processed within 30 days.

## 9. Children

Hang Out is only for people aged **18 or older**, because it is used to meet people in person. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will delete their account. If you believe someone under 18 is using the app, contact us.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict processing. To exercise any right, email **letshangout45@gmail.com**.

## 11. Security

We rely on Google Firebase's security infrastructure and enforce server-side access rules so users can only read and write data they are authorized to. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## 13. Contact

Questions or requests: **letshangout45@gmail.com**.
