# Delete your Hang Out account

This page explains how to delete your **Hang Out** account and the data linked to it. Hang Out is developed by an individual developer and published under the name "Hang Out".

## Option 1: In the app (fastest)

1. Open Hang Out and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Option 2: By email (if you can't use the app)

Send an email to **letshangout45@gmail.com**:

- **Subject:** Delete my account
- **From:** the email address you use to sign in to Hang Out (for Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", mention the relay address shown in your Apple ID settings)

We may reply to confirm that the account belongs to you. We delete the account within **30 days** of receiving your request.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile: name, photo, bio, interests, and your terms acceptance record
- Your sign-in record
- Your friend connections
- Your membership in activities and circles you joined, including your likes and ratings

## What is kept

- **Activities and circles you created**, and **messages you sent** in group chats, stay visible to the other members so their plans and conversations still work. They are no longer linked to a profile.
- **Reports** you submitted or that concern you, and your **block list**, may be kept for as long as needed to handle them and keep users safe.
- **Analytics data** (pseudonymous app-usage events, not linked to your name or email) is deleted automatically by Google Analytics after the retention period set in our Analytics settings.

For more details, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy).
